### 📈 Performance Fee Mechanism

Performance fee dihitung berdasarkan **incremental profit attribution** dari channel AI search:

```
Formula:
✅ Revenue Attribution = Total revenue from customers mentioning AI/organic search in first month
✅ Cost Attribution = COGS + variable costs attributable to those sales
✅ Incremental Profit = Revenue Attribution - Cost Attribution - Baseline profit
✅ Performance Fee = 10-30% × Incremental Profit

Rate Tier:
- 10% → jika conversion rate > industry average 2x
- 20% → jika conversion rate 1.5-2x industry average
- 30% → jika conversion rate < industry average atau trial period
```

**Example scenario:**
```
Baseline monthly profit:        Rp 200,000,000
After GEO implementation:        Rp 270,000,000
Incremental profit:              Rp 70,000,000
Performance fee (20%):           Rp 14,000,000
```
